Both halves,
on purpose.

Five years building production services and the interfaces they power. Three of them at Amazon on Alexa, two at Alphaledger writing the GraphQL layer in the morning and the React consuming it in the afternoon. Seattle-based, open to new roles.

How I work

I stopped picking a side.

Plenty of engineers describe themselves as full stack and mean they can read the other half. I ship on both. At Alphaledger that means designing the schema and the resolvers, then building the AG Grid views that hit them — and reviewing other engineers’ frontend changes along the way.

It also changes the decisions. Knowing what the grid needs shapes the resolver; knowing what the query costs shapes the view. When I bring the team a tooling recommendation it comes with a prototype attached, not an opinion.
