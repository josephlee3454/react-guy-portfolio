Before you write

Three things that make a first message easy to answer.

  1. 01

    What already exists

    A repo, a Figma, a spreadsheet, or nothing at all. All fine — I just need to know which.

  2. 02

    What "done" looks like

    One sentence. If you can't write it yet, say that instead; it's useful information.

  3. 03

    When it matters

    A real date beats "ASAP". If there's no deadline, that changes what I'd recommend.
